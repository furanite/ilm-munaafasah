## 100
The science of eloquent Arabic, fitting speech to its situation. :: Balagha || H: Durus al-Balaghah
The three branches of balagha. :: Ma'ani, bayan, badi' || H: Talkhis al-Miftah (al-Qazwini)
A comparison of one thing with another, like "he is like a lion". :: Tashbih (simile) || H: Durus al-Balaghah
A comparison with the "like" word and one side dropped, "a lion spoke". :: Isti'arah (metaphor) || H: Durus al-Balaghah
Saying one thing but meaning something linked to it, like "long of sword-belt" for tall. :: Kinayah || H: Durus al-Balaghah
Words that sound alike but differ in meaning. :: Jinas || H: Durus al-Balaghah
Putting opposites together, like day and night. :: Tibaq || H: Durus al-Balaghah
Rhyming endings in prose. :: Saj' || H: Durus al-Balaghah
The four parts of a tashbih include the two things compared, the tool and this. :: The shared quality (wajh al-shabah) || H: Durus al-Balaghah
The thing compared in a tashbih. :: Mushabbah || H: Durus al-Balaghah
The thing it is compared to. :: Mushabbah bihi || H: Durus al-Balaghah
"Like" (ka-) and "mithl" are examples of this. :: Adat al-tashbih || H: Durus al-Balaghah
The branch of balagha about beautifying speech. :: 'Ilm al-badi' || H: Talkhis al-Miftah
The branch about expressing one meaning in different clear ways. :: 'Ilm al-bayan || H: Talkhis al-Miftah
The branch about fitting the sentence to the situation. :: 'Ilm al-ma'ani || H: Talkhis al-Miftah
A sentence that can be true or false. :: Khabar || H: Durus al-Balaghah
A sentence that cannot be true or false, like a command or question. :: Insha' || H: Durus al-Balaghah
Speaking briefly with full meaning. :: Ijaz || H: Durus al-Balaghah
Speaking at length for a purpose. :: Itnab || H: Durus al-Balaghah
The modern school textbook of balagha by 'Ali al-Jarim and Mustafa Amin. :: al-Balaghah al-Wadihah || H: al-Balaghah al-Wadihah
In "iyyaka na'budu", putting the object first gives this meaning. :: Restriction ("You alone") || H: Mukhtasar al-Ma'ani (al-Taftazani)
"They are a garment for you" (2:187) is this kind of figure. :: Tashbih baligh || H: al-Balaghah al-Wadihah

## 200
A tashbih with both the tool and the shared quality dropped, the strongest kind. :: Tashbih baligh || H: Durus al-Balaghah
A tashbih that compares a whole picture with a whole picture. :: Tashbih tamthili || H: Durus al-Balaghah
A metaphor that names the thing compared to, dropping the thing compared. :: Isti'arah tasrihiyyah || H: Durus al-Balaghah
A metaphor that drops the thing compared to, keeping one of its features. :: Isti'arah makniyyah || H: Durus al-Balaghah
Using a word for something related, like "hand" for favour. :: Majaz mursal || H: Durus al-Balaghah
Using a word in its first meaning. :: Haqiqah || H: Durus al-Balaghah
Using a word outside its first meaning, with a link. :: Majaz || H: Durus al-Balaghah
Putting a part for the whole, like "eye" for a spy. :: Juz'iyyah (part for whole) || H: Durus al-Balaghah
Speaking of the place for its people, like "the village asked". :: Mahalliyyah || H: Durus al-Balaghah
Words alike in letters, order and vowels, but different meanings. :: Jinas tamm || H: Durus al-Balaghah
Words alike but not fully, differing in one feature. :: Jinas naqis || H: Durus al-Balaghah
Putting two meanings side by side and then their opposites in order. :: Muqabalah || H: Durus al-Balaghah
Giving a reason for something that is not its real reason, in a beautiful way. :: Husn al-ta'lil || H: Durus al-Balaghah
Saying something with two meanings, a near and a far, and meaning the far one. :: Tawriyah || H: Durus al-Balaghah
Praise that sounds like blame. :: Ta'kid al-madh bima yushbih al-dhamm || H: Durus al-Balaghah
Putting a word first that usually comes later, to show focus. :: Taqdim || H: Talkhis al-Miftah
Limiting, as in "innama" or "ma ... illa". :: Qasr (hasr) || H: Durus al-Balaghah
Leaving words out because they are understood. :: Hadhf || H: Durus al-Balaghah
Joining sentences with "wa". :: Wasl || H: Talkhis al-Miftah
Leaving sentences unjoined. :: Fasl || H: Talkhis al-Miftah
"You would think them awake while they are asleep" (18:18) puts opposites together; this figure. :: Tibaq || H: al-Balaghah al-Wadihah
"Let them laugh a little and weep much" (9:82) pairs two against two; this figure. :: Muqabalah || H: al-Balaghah al-Wadihah

Asking a question not to learn but to show surprise or denial. :: Istifham inkari (rhetorical question) || H: Durus al-Balaghah
A command that is not really a command, but a request from a lower to a higher. :: Du'a' (supplication) || H: Durus al-Balaghah
A command from equal to equal. :: Iltimas || H: Durus al-Balaghah
A command that means "do it if you can". :: Ta'jiz (showing inability) || H: Durus al-Balaghah
A switch in the person spoken of, like from "He" to "You". :: Iltifat || H: Talkhis al-Miftah

Repeating a word or idea to stress it. :: Takrar || H: Durus al-Balaghah
"Lower to them the wing of humility" (17:24) uses this figure. :: Isti'arah (makniyyah) || H: al-Balaghah al-Wadihah
"And the head has blazed with grey hair" (19:4) uses this figure. :: Isti'arah || H: al-Balaghah al-Wadihah
