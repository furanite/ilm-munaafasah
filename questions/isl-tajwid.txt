## 100
The science of reciting the Qur'an correctly, giving each letter its due. :: Tajwid || H: al-Muqaddimah al-Jazariyyah
The word "tajwid" comes from a root meaning this. :: To make good or excellent || H: Lisan al-'Arab
Seeking refuge with Allah from Shaytan before reciting is called this. :: Isti'adhah (Ta'awwudh) || H: Qur'an 16:98

The number of qalqalah letters. :: 5 || H: al-Muqaddimah al-Jazariyyah
The phrase that gathers the qalqalah letters. :: Qutb jad || H: al-Muqaddimah al-Jazariyyah
The vowel mark that means a letter has no vowel. :: Sukun || H: Standard term

The verse of Surah al-Muzzammil that commands measured recitation. :: Qur'an 73:4 || H: Qur'an 73:4
The heavy (full-mouthed) way of pronouncing a letter. :: Tafkhim || H: al-Muqaddimah al-Jazariyyah
