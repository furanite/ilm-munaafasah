The supercontinent before Pangaea, about 1 billion years ago. :: Rodinia
The southern supercontinent that included Africa, South America and Antarctica. :: Gondwana
The period of 'Snowball Earth' happened in this era, before animals spread. :: Cryogenian (Neoproterozoic)
The event about 2.4 billion years ago when oxygen first built up in the air. :: Great Oxidation Event
The bacteria that made Earth's early oxygen. :: Cyanobacteria
The layered rocks built by ancient bacterial mats. :: Stromatolites
The hottest temperature reliably recorded on Earth's surface was in this place. :: Death Valley (Furnace Creek)
The coldest natural temperature recorded on Earth's surface was at this Antarctic station. :: Vostok Station
The wind that blows down mountain slopes and warms, as in the Alps. :: Foehn wind
The effect that makes winds curve because of Earth's rotation. :: Coriolis effect
The boundary layer where the troposphere ends. :: Tropopause
The sudden release of a cold air flow from a storm, dangerous to planes. :: Microburst (downburst)
The geologist who wrote Principles of Geology, influencing Darwin. :: Charles Lyell
The idea that the same natural laws shaping the Earth today worked in the past. :: Uniformitarianism
The scientist who founded modern geology with the idea of 'deep time' in 1788. :: James Hutton
The volcanic eruption type with sticky lava and explosive ash columns, like Vesuvius in 79 CE. :: Plinian eruption
The scale used to measure the size of volcanic eruptions. :: Volcanic Explosivity Index (VEI)
The ocean's 'twilight zone', where sunlight fades, is this zone. :: Mesopelagic zone
The deep-sea vents that support life without sunlight. :: Hydrothermal vents (black smokers)
The process by which vent bacteria make food from chemicals. :: Chemosynthesis
The coral's partner algae that live inside its tissues. :: Zooxanthellae
The loss of these algae from coral in hot water is called this. :: Coral bleaching
The number of base pairs in the human genome, roughly. :: About 3 billion
The enzyme that joins DNA fragments together. :: DNA ligase
The cell death programme that removes unwanted cells. :: Apoptosis
The caps at the ends of chromosomes that shorten with age. :: Telomeres
The mineral form of calcium phosphate in bones and teeth. :: Hydroxyapatite
The enzyme in blood that speeds up turning carbon dioxide into bicarbonate. :: Carbonic anhydrase
The light-sensitive protein in the rod cells of the eye. :: Rhodopsin
