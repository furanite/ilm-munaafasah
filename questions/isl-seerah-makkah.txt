# Dates: hijri first (BH = before the Hijrah), then CE. The Prophet ﷺ and His ﷺ Companions are never drawn or voiced.

## 100
The city where our Beloved Prophet ﷺ was born. :: Makkah || H: Sirat Ibn Hisham
The year of his birth is known by this name. :: The Year of the Elephant || H: Sirat Ibn Hisham
The month of his birth, by the most famous view. :: Rabi' al-Awwal || H: Sirat Ibn Hisham
The father of our Beloved Master ﷺ, who died before He ﷺ was born. :: 'Abdullah ibn 'Abd al-Muttalib || H: Sirat Ibn Hisham
The mother of our Beloved Prophet ﷺ. :: Aminah bint Wahb || H: Sirat Ibn Hisham
The wet-nurse of our Beloved Prophet ﷺ in the desert. :: Halimah al-Sa'diyyah (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهَا) || H: Sirat Ibn Hisham
The grandfather who cared for him after his mother died. :: 'Abd al-Muttalib || H: Sirat Ibn Hisham
The uncle who raised and protected him. :: Abu Talib || H: Sirat Ibn Hisham
The title the people of Makkah gave him for his honesty. :: al-Amin (the Trustworthy) || H: Sirat Ibn Hisham
Our Beloved Master's ﷺ first wife, a noble merchant woman. :: Our lady Khadijah bint Khuwaylid (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهَا) || H: Sirat Ibn Hisham
The age at which the first revelation came. :: 40 || H: Sahih al-Bukhari
The first man (adult free man) to accept Islam. :: Our master Abu Bakr al-Siddiq (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهِ) || H: Sirat Ibn Hisham
The first child to accept Islam. :: Our master 'Ali ibn Abi Talib (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهِ) || H: Sirat Ibn Hisham
The first woman to accept Islam. :: Our lady Khadijah (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهَا) || H: Sirat Ibn Hisham
The tribe of our Beloved Master ﷺ. :: Quraysh || H: Sirat Ibn Hisham
Our Beloved Prophet's ﷺ clan within Quraysh. :: Banu Hashim || H: Sirat Ibn Hisham
The first muezzin, who was tortured for his faith in Makkah. :: Bilal ibn Rabah (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهِ) || H: Sirat Ibn Hisham
The night journey from Makkah to Jerusalem. :: al-Isra' || H: Qur'an 17:1
The ascent through the heavens. :: al-Mi'raj || H: Sahih al-Bukhari
The migration to Madinah. :: The Hijrah || H: Sahih al-Bukhari

## 200

Our Beloved Prophet ﷺ was this age when His ﷺ mother died. :: 6 || H: Sirat Ibn Hisham
The place where his mother died, on the way back from Madinah. :: al-Abwa' || H: Sirat Ibn Hisham
Our Beloved Prophet ﷺ was this age when His ﷺ grandfather 'Abd al-Muttalib died. :: 8 || H: Sirat Ibn Hisham
Our Beloved Master ﷺ worked as a young man looking after these. :: Sheep || H: Sahih al-Bukhari
Our Beloved Prophet ﷺ was this age when He ﷺ married our lady Khadijah (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهَا). :: 25 || H: Sirat Ibn Hisham
The servant of Khadijah who travelled with him on her trade to al-Sham. :: Maysarah || H: Sirat Ibn Hisham

For about this many years, the call to Islam was private. :: 3 || H: Sirat Ibn Hisham
The hill from which he called Quraysh openly. :: al-Safa || H: Sahih al-Bukhari
The house in Makkah where early Muslims met in secret. :: Dar al-Arqam || H: Sirat Ibn Hisham
The first martyr in Islam, the mother of 'Ammar. :: Sumayyah (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهَا) || H: Sirat Ibn Hisham
The country the first Muslims migrated to. :: Abyssinia (al-Habashah) || H: Sirat Ibn Hisham
The just Christian king of Abyssinia who protected them. :: al-Najashi (Ashamah) || H: Sahih al-Bukhari; Sirat Ibn Hisham
The Companion who spoke for the Muslims before al-Najashi. :: Ja'far ibn Abi Talib (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهِ) || H: Musnad Ahmad (report of Umm Salamah)
The surah Ja'far (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهِ) recited to al-Najashi. :: Surah Maryam || H: Musnad Ahmad (report of Umm Salamah)
The boycott of Banu Hashim lasted about this many years. :: 3 || H: Sirat Ibn Hisham
The valley where Banu Hashim were besieged. :: Shi'b Abi Talib || H: Sirat Ibn Hisham
The "Year of Sorrow" was when these two died. :: Our lady Khadijah (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهَا) and Abu Talib || H: Sirat Ibn Hisham

## 300
The town our Beloved Prophet ﷺ visited to call its people, where He ﷺ was stoned. :: al-Ta'if || H: Sahih al-Bukhari; Sirat Ibn Hisham
The Christian boy from Nineveh who offered him grapes at Ta'if. :: 'Addas || H: Sirat Ibn Hisham
The animal he rode on the Isra'. :: al-Buraq || H: Sahih al-Bukhari
On the Mi'raj, the prayers were reduced from fifty to this many. :: 5 || H: Sahih al-Bukhari
The Prophet who advised him on the Mi'raj to ask for fewer prayers. :: Musa (عليه السلام) || H: Sahih al-Bukhari
The Companion who believed the Isra' at once and got the title "al-Siddiq". :: Our master Abu Bakr (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهِ) || H: al-Mustadrak (al-Hakim)
The first pledge at 'Aqabah was made by this many people of Yathrib. :: 12 || H: Sirat Ibn Hisham

The second pledge of 'Aqabah was made by about this many men and two women. :: 73 || H: Sirat Ibn Hisham
The two tribes of Yathrib. :: al-Aws and al-Khazraj || H: Sirat Ibn Hisham
The house where Quraysh met to plot against our Beloved Prophet ﷺ. :: Dar al-Nadwah || H: Sirat Ibn Hisham
The Companion who slept in our Beloved Prophet's ﷺ bed on the night of the Hijrah. :: Our master 'Ali ibn Abi Talib (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهِ) || H: Sirat Ibn Hisham
The cave where our Beloved Master ﷺ and our master Abu Bakr (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهِ) hid for three nights. :: The Cave of Thawr || H: Sahih al-Bukhari
The verse "Do not grieve; Allah is with us" (meaning) is in this surah. :: Surah al-Tawbah (9:40) || H: Qur'an 9:40
The daughter of Abu Bakr called "Dhat al-Nitaqayn". :: Asma' bint Abi Bakr (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهَا) || H: Sahih al-Bukhari
The guide they hired for the Hijrah journey. :: 'Abdullah ibn Urayqit || H: Sahih al-Bukhari (named in Sirat Ibn Hisham)
The rider who chased them and whose horse sank into the sand. :: Suraqah ibn Malik (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهِ) || H: Sahih al-Bukhari
The woman whose dry goat gave milk when our Beloved Prophet ﷺ passed her tent. :: Umm Ma'bad (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهَا) || H?: al-Mustadrak (al-Hakim); to be checked
The first mosque built in Islam. :: Masjid Quba' || H: Sahih al-Bukhari

## 400

The master who tortured Bilal (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهِ) on the hot sand. :: Umayyah ibn Khalaf || H: Sirat Ibn Hisham
The word Bilal (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهِ) repeated under torture. :: Ahad, ahad (One, One) || H: Sirat Ibn Hisham

Our master 'Umar (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهِ) accepted Islam after hearing this surah at his sister's house. :: Surah Taha || H: Sirat Ibn Hisham

The Quraysh leader who said the Qur'an was magic after admitting its beauty, and about whom Surah al-Muddaththir speaks. :: al-Walid ibn al-Mughirah || H: Tafsir al-Tabari, Qur'an 74:11-25
The Quraysh envoy sent to bring the Muslims back from Abyssinia, who later became a Companion. :: 'Amr ibn al-'As (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهِ) || H: Musnad Ahmad (report of Umm Salamah)

The man of Ta'if's journey who offered protection when our Beloved Prophet ﷺ returned to Makkah. :: al-Mut'im ibn 'Adi || H: Sahih al-Bukhari; Sirat Ibn Hisham
Our Beloved Prophet ﷺ was offered that the angel of the mountains crush the people of Ta'if; He ﷺ hoped for this instead. :: That their descendants would worship Allah || H: Sahih al-Bukhari
Our Beloved Master ﷺ led the Blessed Prophets (عليهم السلام) in prayer on the Isra' at this mosque. :: al-Masjid al-Aqsa || H: Sahih Muslim

The first pledge of 'Aqabah is also called this, as it had no pledge to fight. :: Bay'at al-Nisa' || H: Sirat Ibn Hisham

Our Beloved Prophet ﷺ arrived at Quba' on this day of the week, by the common view. :: Monday || H: Sahih al-Bukhari; Sirat Ibn Hisham
The son of Abu Bakr who brought them news in the cave each night. :: 'Abdullah ibn Abi Bakr (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهِمَا) || H: Sahih al-Bukhari
The freedman of Abu Bakr who grazed sheep to hide their tracks. :: 'Amir ibn Fuhayrah (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهِ) || H: Sahih al-Bukhari
The reward Quraysh offered for capturing our Beloved Master ﷺ and Abu Bakr. :: 100 camels for each || H: Sahih al-Bukhari
