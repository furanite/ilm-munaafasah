# Islamic and Kids packs: style rules

These rules apply to every file whose `group:` is quran, hadith, aqidah, fiqh, prophets, arabic,
tazkiyah, islamichistory or kids. A scholar should review the packs before they are used in public.

## Respect (always)

- Never depict Prophets or Companions in any form: no figure, silhouette or voice. These packs are
  text only, and their cards are shown "calm" (no spin, glint or particle effects).
- Never show Makkah al-Mukarramah or Madinah al-Munawwarah as a location or background.
- Never damage, shake or misuse Qur'anic text or the Names of Allah.
- All people are faceless and stylised; real modern figures appear only in silhouette or from behind.

## Honorifics

| Who | Write after the name |
| --- | --- |
| The Prophet Muhammad | ﷺ, after every mention, pronouns included ("He ﷺ", "His ﷺ"). Call him "our Beloved Prophet ﷺ", and sometimes "our Beloved Master ﷺ", never just "the Prophet" or "our Prophet". |
| Other Prophets and angels named as such | (عليه السلام). Say "the Blessed Prophet X" or, where it fits, "our Master X". |
| The Prophets as a group | "the Blessed Prophets (عليهم السلام)" |
| Maryam | (عليها السلام) |
| Companions | (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهِ), (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهَا), (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهِمَا), (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهِم), (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهِنَّ) |
| The four Khulafa' and the Blessed Family | as Companions, and "our master" before the name ("our lady" for a woman), for example "our master Abu Bakr", "our lady Fatimah" |
| Sunni scholars | رحمة الله عليه, or رحمة الله عليها for a woman |

Do not add رحمة الله عليه for Wahhabi or Deobandi scholars. Those figures, and modern sectarian
figures, are left out of the packs entirely. Figures whose creed is not Sunni (for example
Mu'tazili grammarians) are named without an honorific. If you are unsure, hold the line back with `?`.

Never change wording inside a quotation of the Qur'an or a hadith to add an honorific; put the honorific after the closing quotation mark.

## Creed and fiqh

- Creed follows the Ash'ari and Maturidi schools.
- Fiqh lines state what the four schools agree on. Where they differ, the clue names the school.
  Main texts cited: Nur al-Idah and al-Hidayah (Hanafi), al-Risalah (Maliki),
  Matn Abi Shuja' (Shafi'i), Zad al-Mustaqni' (Hanbali).

## Accuracy

- Dates: hijri first, then CE, for example `2 AH (624 CE)`.
- Every line ends with a tag: `|| H: source` (historical), `|| D: note` (dramatised or a popular
  report of weak or debated grading), or `|| F: note` (fictional or a teaching example).
- Never invent a quotation. Paraphrase and say so in the clue, for example "(paraphrased)" or
  "(meaning)". Qur'an meanings are given as meanings, never as the Qur'an's words.
- If you are not sure of a fact, use `H?:` (or `D?:`, `F?:`) and say what to check. The line is
  held out of the game and listed in `questions/TO-VERIFY.md` until the `?` is removed.
- English only. Arabic terms and honorifics appear inline; do not machine-translate whole lines.

## Pool size

Religious packs aim for at least 50 playable lines per level in every sub-category (the owner's
request of 2026-11-11). The build itself only requires one.
