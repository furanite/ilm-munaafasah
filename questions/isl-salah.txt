## 300
In the Hanafi school, witr is this ruling. :: Wajib || H: Nur al-Idah (al-Shurunbulali)
In the Shafi'i, Maliki and Hanbali schools, witr is this ruling. :: A stressed sunnah || H: Matn Abi Shuja'; al-Risalah; Zad al-Mustaqni'
In the Hanafi school, witr is prayed with this many rak'ahs. :: 3 (with one taslim) || H: Nur al-Idah (al-Shurunbulali)
In the Hanafi school, the qunut of witr is said here. :: Before ruku' in the third rak'ah || H: Nur al-Idah (al-Shurunbulali)
In the Shafi'i school, qunut is said in this daily prayer. :: Fajr || H: Matn Abi Shuja'
In the Hanafi school, reciting al-Fatihah is this ruling in prayer. :: Wajib (the fard is reciting some Qur'an) || H: Nur al-Idah (al-Shurunbulali)
In the Shafi'i school, reciting al-Fatihah is this. :: A pillar (rukn) || H: Matn Abi Shuja'
In the Hanafi school, the follower behind the imam does this regarding recitation. :: Stays silent || H: Nur al-Idah (al-Shurunbulali)
In the Shafi'i school, the follower does this regarding al-Fatihah. :: Recites it || H: Matn Abi Shuja'
In the Hanafi school, the travel distance for shortening prayers is about this many days' journey. :: 3 days (about 77 to 88 km by modern estimates) || H: Nur al-Idah (al-Shurunbulali); modern estimates differ
In the Shafi'i, Maliki and Hanbali schools, the distance for shortening is this many barids. :: 4 barids (about 80 to 89 km) || H: Matn Abi Shuja'; Zad al-Mustaqni'
In the Hanafi school, joining two prayers in the time of one is allowed only here. :: At 'Arafah and Muzdalifah in Hajj || H: Nur al-Idah (al-Shurunbulali)
In the Hanafi school, shortening the prayer while travelling is this. :: Wajib || H: Nur al-Idah (al-Shurunbulali)
In the Shafi'i school, shortening the prayer while travelling is this. :: Allowed (a concession) || H: Matn Abi Shuja'
The Hanafi school ends 'Asr's start when an object's shadow is this long, by Abu Hanifah's view. :: Twice its length (plus the noon shadow) || H: Nur al-Idah (al-Shurunbulali)
The majority start 'Asr when an object's shadow equals this. :: Its length (plus the noon shadow) || H: Matn Abi Shuja'; Zad al-Mustaqni'
The times when voluntary prayer is forbidden include sunrise, sunset and this. :: When the sun is at its zenith || H: Sahih Muslim
Our Beloved Master ﷺ said "Pray as you have seen me pray" (meaning) to this group. :: Malik ibn al-Huwayrith and his companions || H: Sahih al-Bukhari
The man our Beloved Prophet ﷺ told "Go back and pray, for you have not prayed" (meaning). :: The man who prayed badly (al-musi' salatahu) || H: Sahih al-Bukhari
The Companion who saw the words of the adhan in a dream. :: 'Abdullah ibn Zayd (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهِ) || H: Sunan Abi Dawud

## 400
In the Hanafi school, the wajib acts of prayer, if missed by mistake, are made up by this. :: Sujud al-sahw || H: Nur al-Idah (al-Shurunbulali)
In the Hanafi school, sujud al-sahw is done at this point. :: After one taslim at the end || H: Nur al-Idah (al-Shurunbulali)
In the Shafi'i school, sujud al-sahw is done at this point. :: Before the taslim || H: Matn Abi Shuja'
In the Maliki school, sujud al-sahw for adding something is done here. :: After the taslim || H: al-Risalah (Ibn Abi Zayd)
In the Maliki school, sujud al-sahw for leaving something out is done here. :: Before the taslim || H: al-Risalah (Ibn Abi Zayd)
In the Hanafi school, the least number of men for Jumu'ah besides the imam. :: 3 || H: Nur al-Idah (al-Shurunbulali)
In the Shafi'i and Hanbali schools, the least number for Jumu'ah. :: 40 || H: Matn Abi Shuja'; Zad al-Mustaqni'
In the Hanafi school, the funeral prayer has this many pillars. :: 2 (the takbirs and standing) || H: Nur al-Idah (al-Shurunbulali)
In the Hanafi school, the number of extra takbirs in each rak'ah of Eid. :: 3 || H: Nur al-Idah (al-Shurunbulali)
In the Shafi'i school, the number of extra takbirs in the first rak'ah of Eid. :: 7 || H: Matn Abi Shuja'
In the Shafi'i school, the number of extra takbirs in the second rak'ah of Eid. :: 5 || H: Matn Abi Shuja'
In the Hanafi school, the Eid prayer is this ruling. :: Wajib || H: Nur al-Idah (al-Shurunbulali)
In the Hanafi school, the hands are placed here in standing, for men. :: Below the navel || H: Nur al-Idah (al-Shurunbulali)
In the Shafi'i school, the hands are placed here in standing. :: Below the chest, above the navel || H: Matn Abi Shuja'; al-Minhaj
In the Maliki school, the famous view on the hands in fard prayers. :: Leaving them at the sides (sadl) || H: al-Risalah (Ibn Abi Zayd)
In the Hanafi school, raising the hands in prayer is done only here. :: At the opening takbir || H: Nur al-Idah (al-Shurunbulali)
In the Shafi'i school, the hands are also raised at these two points. :: Going into ruku' and rising from it || H: Sahih al-Bukhari; Matn Abi Shuja'
In the Shafi'i school, the number of pillars (arkan) of prayer, by a common count. :: 17 || H?: Safinat al-Naja; counts differ in Shafi'i texts
Our Beloved Prophet ﷺ prayed the "fear prayer" (salat al-khawf) in this many ways, by reports. :: Several (scholars count about six to sixteen) || H?: Sunan Abi Dawud; to be checked
The verse of the fear prayer is in this surah. :: Surah al-Nisa' (4:102) || H: Qur'an 4:102
