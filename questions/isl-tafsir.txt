## 100
The science of explaining the meanings of the Qur'an. :: Tafsir || H: al-Itqan (al-Suyuti)
The sciences about the Qur'an, its revelation, collection and reading, together called this. :: 'Ulum al-Qur'an || H: al-Itqan (al-Suyuti)
A scholar who explains the Qur'an. :: Mufassir || H: Standard term
The "occasions of revelation", the events that verses came down about. :: Asbab al-Nuzul || H: Asbab al-Nuzul (al-Wahidi)
The Companion called "Tarjuman al-Qur'an", the interpreter of the Qur'an. :: 'Abdullah ibn 'Abbas (رضي الله عنه) || H: al-Itqan (al-Suyuti)
The Prophet ﷺ prayed that Allah teach this Companion understanding of the religion and interpretation (paraphrased). :: 'Abdullah ibn 'Abbas (رضي الله عنه) || H: Sahih al-Bukhari; Musnad Ahmad
Explaining the Qur'an by the Qur'an, the Sunnah and the Companions is called this kind of tafsir. :: Tafsir bil-ma'thur || H: al-Itqan (al-Suyuti)
Explaining the Qur'an by sound reasoning within the rules is called this. :: Tafsir bil-ra'y (al-mahmud) || H: al-Itqan (al-Suyuti)
The famous short tafsir written by two scholars both named Jalal al-Din. :: Tafsir al-Jalalayn || H: Tafsir al-Jalalayn
The two authors of Tafsir al-Jalalayn. :: Jalal al-Din al-Mahalli and Jalal al-Din al-Suyuti رحمة الله عليهما || H: Tafsir al-Jalalayn
The great early tafsir by Ibn Jarir al-Tabari رحمة الله عليه. :: Jami' al-Bayan || H: Jami' al-Bayan (al-Tabari)
A verse revealed in Makkah is called this. :: Makki || H: al-Itqan (al-Suyuti)
A verse revealed in Madinah is called this. :: Madani || H: al-Itqan (al-Suyuti)
The first verses ever revealed. :: The opening verses of Surah al-'Alaq (96:1-5) || H: Sahih al-Bukhari, Book of Revelation
A verse is called this when it replaces the ruling of an earlier one. :: Nasikh || H: al-Itqan (al-Suyuti)
The verse whose ruling was replaced is called this. :: Mansukh || H: al-Itqan (al-Suyuti)
The first thing the Prophet ﷺ saw of revelation was this (paraphrased from 'A'ishah (رضي الله عنها)). :: True dreams || H: Sahih al-Bukhari, Book of Revelation
Putting the meanings of the Qur'an into another language is called this. :: Tarjamat al-ma'ani (translation of the meanings) || H: Standard term
The Arabic word "wahy" means this. :: Revelation || H: Lisan al-'Arab
The Qur'an came down to the lowest heaven all at once on Laylat al-Qadr, according to this Companion. :: 'Abdullah ibn 'Abbas (رضي الله عنه) || H: al-Mustadrak (al-Hakim); al-Itqan (al-Suyuti)
