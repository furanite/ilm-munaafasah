## 100
The science of the terms and rules used to judge hadith. :: Mustalah al-Hadith || H: Nukhbat al-Fikar (Ibn Hajar)
The chain of narrators of a hadith. :: Isnad (sanad) || H: Nukhbat al-Fikar (Ibn Hajar)
The text of a hadith, after the chain. :: Matn || H: Nukhbat al-Fikar (Ibn Hajar)
A hadith that is sound, the highest grade. :: Sahih || H: Muqaddimah Ibn al-Salah
A hadith that is good, a little below sahih. :: Hasan || H: Muqaddimah Ibn al-Salah
A hadith that is weak. :: Da'if || H: Muqaddimah Ibn al-Salah
A fabricated report falsely called a hadith. :: Mawdu' || H: Muqaddimah Ibn al-Salah
A report narrated by so many that they could not have agreed on a lie. :: Mutawatir || H: Nukhbat al-Fikar (Ibn Hajar)
A report that does not reach the level of mutawatir. :: Ahad || H: Nukhbat al-Fikar (Ibn Hajar)
A hadith in which Allah's words are narrated by our Beloved Prophet ﷺ in His ﷺ own wording, outside the Qur'an. :: Hadith qudsi || H: Standard term
A narrator who is trustworthy and precise. :: Thiqah || H: Taqrib al-Tahdhib (Ibn Hajar)
The science of praising and criticising narrators. :: al-Jarh wal-Ta'dil || H: al-Jarh wal-Ta'dil (Ibn Abi Hatim)
A narration that goes back to our Beloved Prophet ﷺ Himself ﷺ. :: Marfu' || H: Muqaddimah Ibn al-Salah
A narration that stops at a Companion. :: Mawquf || H: Muqaddimah Ibn al-Salah
A narration that stops at a Successor. :: Maqtu' || H: Muqaddimah Ibn al-Salah
Our Beloved Master ﷺ warned that whoever lies about Him ﷺ on purpose should take his seat in the Fire (paraphrased). This hadith's grade by transmission. :: Mutawatir || H: Muqaddimah Ibn al-Salah
The person who narrates a hadith is called this. :: Rawi || H: Standard term
A scholar who has mastered hadith is called this. :: Muhaddith || H: Standard term
The short poem on hadith terms by al-Bayquni رحمة الله عليه. :: al-Manzumah al-Bayquniyyah || H: al-Bayquniyyah
The short book on hadith terms by Ibn Hajar al-'Asqalani رحمة الله عليه. :: Nukhbat al-Fikar || H: Nukhbat al-Fikar
Reports from the Companions and Successors are often called this. :: Athar || H: Tadrib al-Rawi (al-Suyuti)
The generation after the Successors. :: Atba' al-Tabi'in || H: Muqaddimat Ibn al-Salah

## 200
The number of conditions for a hadith to be sahih. :: 5 || H: Muqaddimah Ibn al-Salah
The condition that every narrator heard from the one before him. :: Ittisal al-sanad (a connected chain) || H: Muqaddimah Ibn al-Salah
The condition that every narrator is upright in religion. :: 'Adalah || H: Muqaddimah Ibn al-Salah
The condition that every narrator is precise in memory or writing. :: Dabt || H: Muqaddimah Ibn al-Salah
The two conditions of sahih that rule out hidden faults and odd reports. :: No 'illah and no shudhudh || H: Muqaddimah Ibn al-Salah
A hadith narrated by a Successor straight from our Beloved Prophet ﷺ, leaving out the Companion. :: Mursal || H: Muqaddimah Ibn al-Salah
A chain with a narrator missing somewhere in the middle. :: Munqati' || H: Muqaddimah Ibn al-Salah
A chain with two or more narrators missing in a row. :: Mu'dal || H: Muqaddimah Ibn al-Salah
A chain with one or more narrators dropped at its start (the compiler's end). :: Mu'allaq || H: Muqaddimah Ibn al-Salah
An ahad report narrated by at least three at each level, short of tawatur. :: Mashhur || H: Nukhbat al-Fikar (Ibn Hajar)
An ahad report narrated by no fewer than two at each level. :: 'Aziz || H: Nukhbat al-Fikar (Ibn Hajar)
A report narrated by only one at some level. :: Gharib || H: Nukhbat al-Fikar (Ibn Hajar)
A weak hadith that rises because of supporting chains. :: Hasan li-ghayrihi || H: Nukhbat al-Fikar (Ibn Hajar)
A hasan hadith that rises because of supporting chains. :: Sahih li-ghayrihi || H: Nukhbat al-Fikar (Ibn Hajar)
A hadith where the narrator conceals a gap in the chain, as by saying "from" instead of "he told me" (meaning). :: Mudallas || H: Muqaddimah Ibn al-Salah
The practice of hiding such a gap. :: Tadlis || H: Muqaddimah Ibn al-Salah
A hadith with words of a narrator mixed into the text. :: Mudraj || H: Muqaddimah Ibn al-Salah
A narrator accused of lying, whose hadith are dropped. :: Matruk || H: Nukhbat al-Fikar (Ibn Hajar)
A hadith with an unbroken chain to our Beloved Prophet ﷺ. :: Musnad || H: Nukhbat al-Fikar (Ibn Hajar)
The weak narrator's report that goes against a reliable narrator. :: Munkar || H: Nukhbat al-Fikar (Ibn Hajar)

## 300
A report by a reliable narrator that goes against those more reliable. :: Shadh || H: Nukhbat al-Fikar (Ibn Hajar)
A report with a hidden flaw that only experts can see. :: Mu'allal (ma'lul) || H: Muqaddimah Ibn al-Salah
A hadith whose chain or text has been switched around by mistake. :: Maqlub || H: Muqaddimah Ibn al-Salah
A hadith narrated in conflicting ways of equal strength. :: Mudtarib || H: Muqaddimah Ibn al-Salah
A hadith where a word is changed in its dots. :: Musahhaf || H: Nukhbat al-Fikar (Ibn Hajar)
A hadith where a word is changed in its shape or vowels. :: Muharraf || H: Nukhbat al-Fikar (Ibn Hajar)
A short chain with few narrators between the compiler and our Beloved Master ﷺ. :: 'Ali (high) isnad || H: Muqaddimah Ibn al-Salah
A long chain with more narrators. :: Nazil (low) isnad || H: Muqaddimah Ibn al-Salah
A chain in which every narrator says the same words or does the same act. :: Musalsal || H: Muqaddimah Ibn al-Salah
The famous musalsal hadith on mercy that scholars read first to students. :: al-Musalsal bil-awwaliyyah || H: Fath al-Bari; works of thabat
A narrator who lived in both Jahiliyyah and Islam but did not meet our Beloved Prophet ﷺ as a believer. :: Mukhadram || H: Muqaddimah Ibn al-Salah
A supporting chain for the same Companion. :: Mutabi' || H: Nukhbat al-Fikar (Ibn Hajar)
A supporting report of the same meaning from a different Companion. :: Shahid || H: Nukhbat al-Fikar (Ibn Hajar)
The study of searching for supporting chains. :: al-I'tibar || H: Nukhbat al-Fikar (Ibn Hajar)
Hadith of a narrator who mixed up his memory late in life. :: Mukhtalit || H: Nukhbat al-Fikar (Ibn Hajar)
The eight ways of receiving hadith include sama', the first, which means this. :: Hearing from the teacher || H: Muqaddimah Ibn al-Salah
The way of receiving where the student reads to the teacher. :: Qira'ah ('ard) || H: Muqaddimah Ibn al-Salah
The permission a teacher gives to narrate his books. :: Ijazah || H: Muqaddimah Ibn al-Salah
The way of receiving where the teacher hands over his written book. :: Munawalah || H: Muqaddimah Ibn al-Salah
The way of receiving through a letter from the teacher. :: Mukatabah || H: Muqaddimah Ibn al-Salah
The highest of the levels of sahih hadith. :: Those narrated by both al-Bukhari and Muslim رحمة الله عليهماا || H: Nuzhat al-Nazar (Ibn Hajar)
A hadith like sahih, but whose narrators' precision is a little lighter. :: Hasan li-dhatihi || H: Nuzhat al-Nazar (Ibn Hajar)
A narrator in a chain who is mentioned but not named, like "a man". :: Mubham || H: Nuzhat al-Nazar (Ibn Hajar)
'Abdullah ibn al-Mubarak رحمة الله عليه said the isnad is part of this. :: The religion || H: Muqaddimat Sahih Muslim
Muhammad ibn Sirin رحمة الله عليه said this knowledge is religion, so look at this. :: From whom you take your religion || H: Muqaddimat Sahih Muslim

## 400
The most famous book of hadith terms, by Ibn al-Salah رحمة الله عليه (d. 643 AH / 1245 CE). :: 'Ulum al-Hadith (Muqaddimah Ibn al-Salah) || H: Muqaddimah Ibn al-Salah
The first known book on hadith terms, by al-Ramahurmuzi رحمة الله عليه. :: al-Muhaddith al-Fasil || H: al-Muhaddith al-Fasil
The book on hadith sciences by al-Hakim al-Naysaburi رحمة الله عليه. :: Ma'rifat 'Ulum al-Hadith || H: Ma'rifat 'Ulum al-Hadith
The book on the rules of narration by al-Khatib al-Baghdadi رحمة الله عليه. :: al-Kifayah fi 'Ilm al-Riwayah || H: al-Kifayah
The poem in a thousand lines on hadith terms by al-'Iraqi رحمة الله عليه. :: Alfiyyat al-'Iraqi (al-Tabsirah wal-Tadhkirah) || H: Alfiyyat al-'Iraqi
The commentary of al-Suyuti رحمة الله عليه on al-Nawawi's Taqrib. :: Tadrib al-Rawi || H: Tadrib al-Rawi
Ibn Hajar's رحمة الله عليه own commentary on his Nukhbat al-Fikar. :: Nuzhat al-Nazar || H: Nuzhat al-Nazar
The commentary of al-Sakhawi رحمة الله عليه on the Alfiyyah of al-'Iraqi. :: Fath al-Mughith || H: Fath al-Mughith
A Companion's statement about something only known by revelation takes this ruling. :: The ruling of marfu' (hukmuhu al-raf') || H: Nukhbat al-Fikar (Ibn Hajar)
The view of most scholars on all the Companions' reliability. :: All are upright ('udul) || H: al-Isabah (Ibn Hajar), introduction
The highest level of praise for a narrator, such as "most reliable of people" (meaning). :: Awthaq al-nas (af'al form) || H: Nuzhat al-Nazar (Ibn Hajar)
A hadith that al-Bukhari and Muslim رحمة الله عليهماا both narrate from the same Companion. :: Muttafaq 'alayh || H: Standard term
A hadith that meets al-Bukhari's رحمة الله عليه and Muslim's conditions but neither narrated it. :: 'Ala shart al-shaykhayn || H: al-Mustadrak (al-Hakim)
The category of hadith al-Tirmidhi رحمة الله عليه often calls "hasan sahih". :: Both hasan and sahih by different chains or views || H: Nuzhat al-Nazar (Ibn Hajar)
A narrator whose name is known but whose state is not. :: Majhul al-hal (mastur) || H: Nukhbat al-Fikar (Ibn Hajar)
A narrator known by only one student. :: Majhul al-'ayn || H: Nukhbat al-Fikar (Ibn Hajar)
Most scholars accept the narration of a truthful innovator on this condition. :: He does not call others to his innovation || H: Nuzhat al-Nazar (Ibn Hajar)
The rule for acting on a weak hadith in virtuous deeds, under conditions. :: Allowed by most scholars (fada'il al-a'mal) || H: al-Adhkar (al-Nawawi), introduction
"Actions are by intentions" is mass-narrated later in its chain, but at its first levels it is this kind of report (meaning). :: Gharib (fard) || H: Nuzhat al-Nazar (Ibn Hajar)
The number of ways of receiving hadith that Ibn al-Salah رحمة الله عليه lists. :: 8 || H: Muqaddimah Ibn al-Salah
Hadith of al-Bukhari رحمة الله عليه with only three narrators between him and our Beloved Prophet ﷺ are called this. :: Thulathiyyat (there are 22) || H: Fath al-Bari (Ibn Hajar)
An extra word narrated by one reliable narrator that others did not narrate. :: Ziyadat al-thiqah || H: Nuzhat al-Nazar (Ibn Hajar)
Receiving hadith by finding a scholar's writing, without hearing or permission. :: Wijadah || H: Muqaddimat Ibn al-Salah

## 500
The book in which Imam al-Shafi'i رحمة الله عليه set conditions for accepting a mursal hadith. :: al-Risalah (al-Shafi'i) || H: al-Risalah (al-Shafi'i)
The Imam who accepted the mursal of the senior Successors, in line with his school. :: Imam Abu Hanifah رحمة الله عليه (and Malik) || H: Usul al-Sarakhsi
The Successor whose mursal reports al-Shafi'i رحمة الله عليه especially accepted. :: Sa'id ibn al-Musayyib رحمة الله عليه || H: al-Risalah (al-Shafi'i); Muqaddimah Ibn al-Salah
Tadlis where a narrator calls his teacher by an unknown name. :: Tadlis al-shuyukh || H: Muqaddimah Ibn al-Salah
Tadlis where a weak narrator between two reliable ones is dropped. :: Tadlis al-taswiyah || H: Nukhbat al-Fikar (Ibn Hajar)
The book by Ibn Hajar رحمة الله عليه listing narrators known for tadlis. :: Tabaqat al-Mudallisin (Ta'rif Ahl al-Taqdis) || H: Ta'rif Ahl al-Taqdis
The book by al-Daraqutni رحمة الله عليه on the hidden flaws in hadith. :: al-'Ilal || H: al-'Ilal (al-Daraqutni)
The book by al-Daraqutni رحمة الله عليه critiquing hadith in the two Sahihs. :: al-Ilzamat wal-Tatabbu' || H: al-Ilzamat wal-Tatabbu'
Ibn Hajar رحمة الله عليه answered the critiques of al-Bukhari's narrators in this introduction to Fath al-Bari. :: Hady al-Sari || H: Hady al-Sari
The science of narrators with similar names written alike but said differently. :: al-Mu'talif wal-Mukhtalif || H: Muqaddimah Ibn al-Salah
The science of narrators with the same name and father's name who are different people. :: al-Muttafiq wal-Muftariq || H: Muqaddimah Ibn al-Salah
The study of hadith that seem to contradict and how to reconcile them. :: Mukhtalif al-Hadith || H: Ikhtilaf al-Hadith (al-Shafi'i)
The book of Imam al-Shafi'i رحمة الله عليه on reconciling hadith. :: Ikhtilaf al-Hadith || H: Ikhtilaf al-Hadith
The book of al-Tahawi رحمة الله عليه reconciling problematic hadith. :: Sharh Mushkil al-Athar || H: Sharh Mushkil al-Athar
The study of the unusual words in hadith. :: Gharib al-Hadith || H: al-Nihayah (Ibn al-Athir)
The famous dictionary of unusual hadith words by Ibn al-Athir رحمة الله عليه. :: al-Nihayah fi Gharib al-Hadith || H: al-Nihayah
The term for when a student narrates from a teacher younger than himself. :: Riwayat al-akabir 'an al-asaghir || H: Muqaddimah Ibn al-Salah
The term for two peers narrating from each other. :: al-Mudabbaj || H: Muqaddimah Ibn al-Salah
Ibn Hajar's رحمة الله عليه term in al-Taqrib for a narrator whose hadith is accepted only when supported. :: Maqbul || H: Taqrib al-Tahdhib (introduction)
The number of levels (tabaqat) of narrators in Ibn Hajar's رحمة الله عليه Taqrib. :: 12 || H: Taqrib al-Tahdhib (introduction)
Receiving hadith when a teacher leaves his books to someone at his death. :: Wasiyyah || H: Muqaddimat Ibn al-Salah
Abu Dawud رحمة الله عليه said the hadith in his Sunan that he passed over in silence are this. :: Salih (fit to use) || H: Risalat Abi Dawud ila Ahl Makkah
