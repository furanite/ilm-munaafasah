# Every Companion's name carries (رضي الله عنه / عنها / عنهم). No Companion is ever drawn or voiced.

## 100
The first caliph of Islam. :: Abu Bakr al-Siddiq (رضي الله عنه) || H: Sahih al-Bukhari
The second caliph, called al-Faruq. :: 'Umar ibn al-Khattab (رضي الله عنه) || H: Tarikh al-Tabari
The third caliph, called Dhul-Nurayn. :: 'Uthman ibn 'Affan (رضي الله عنه) || H: Tarikh al-Tabari
The fourth caliph, cousin and son-in-law of the Prophet ﷺ. :: 'Ali ibn Abi Talib (رضي الله عنه) || H: Tarikh al-Tabari
The four caliphs together are called this. :: al-Khulafa' al-Rashidun || H: Sunan Abi Dawud (hadith of al-'Irbad)
The ten Companions given glad tidings of Paradise by name. :: al-'Asharah al-Mubashsharah || H: Sunan al-Tirmidhi
The Companion who gave the first call to prayer. :: Bilal ibn Rabah (رضي الله عنه) || H: Sunan Abi Dawud
The daughter of the Prophet ﷺ, wife of 'Ali (رضي الله عنه). :: Fatimah al-Zahra' (رضي الله عنها) || H: Sahih al-Bukhari
The two grandsons of the Prophet ﷺ. :: al-Hasan and al-Husayn (رضي الله عنهما) || H: Sahih al-Bukhari
The Prophet ﷺ called them "the leaders of the youth of Paradise" (paraphrased). :: al-Hasan and al-Husayn (رضي الله عنهما) || H: Sunan al-Tirmidhi
The Companion called "Sayf Allah" (Sword of Allah). :: Khalid ibn al-Walid (رضي الله عنه) || H: Sahih al-Bukhari
The Persian Companion who searched for the truth across lands. :: Salman al-Farisi (رضي الله عنه) || H: Musnad Ahmad
The Companion called "Amin hadhihi al-Ummah" (trustee of this Ummah). :: Abu 'Ubaydah ibn al-Jarrah (رضي الله عنه) || H: Sahih al-Bukhari
The wives of the Prophet ﷺ are called this. :: Ummahat al-Mu'minin (Mothers of the Believers) || H: Qur'an 33:6
The young Companion who became a great scholar of tafsir. :: 'Abdullah ibn 'Abbas (رضي الله عنهما) || H: Sahih al-Bukhari
The Companion who narrated the most hadith. :: Abu Hurayrah (رضي الله عنه) || H: Siyar A'lam al-Nubala' (al-Dhahabi)
The Prophet's servant for about ten years. :: Anas ibn Malik (رضي الله عنه) || H: Sahih Muslim
The Companion the Prophet ﷺ called "the most knowledgeable of halal and haram" (paraphrased). :: Mu'adh ibn Jabal (رضي الله عنه) || H: Sunan al-Tirmidhi
The uncle of the Prophet ﷺ called "Asad Allah" (Lion of Allah). :: Hamzah (رضي الله عنه) || H: al-Mustadrak (al-Hakim)
The Companion who was the first ambassador of Islam, sent to teach Yathrib. :: Mus'ab ibn 'Umayr (رضي الله عنه) || H: Sirat Ibn Hisham

## 200
The ten promised Paradise include the four caliphs, Talhah, al-Zubayr, 'Abd al-Rahman ibn 'Awf, Sa'd, Sa'id and this one. :: Abu 'Ubaydah ibn al-Jarrah (رضي الله عنه) || H: Sunan al-Tirmidhi
The Companion who was the first to shoot an arrow in the way of Allah. :: Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas (رضي الله عنه) || H: Sahih al-Bukhari
The commander of the Muslims at al-Qadisiyyah. :: Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas (رضي الله عنه) || H: Tarikh al-Tabari
The Companion called the "disciple" (hawari) of the Prophet ﷺ. :: al-Zubayr ibn al-'Awwam (رضي الله عنه) || H: Sahih al-Bukhari
The wealthy Companion who began life in Madinah asking to be shown the market. :: 'Abd al-Rahman ibn 'Awf (رضي الله عنه) || H: Sahih al-Bukhari
The Companion who shielded the Prophet ﷺ with his hand at Uhud, which was left paralysed. :: Talhah ibn 'Ubaydullah (رضي الله عنه) || H: Sahih al-Bukhari
The Companion who was the scribe of revelation and collected the Qur'an. :: Zayd ibn Thabit (رضي الله عنه) || H: Sahih al-Bukhari
The Companion whose recitation the Prophet ﷺ loved and asked to hear. :: 'Abdullah ibn Mas'ud (رضي الله عنه) || H: Sahih al-Bukhari
The Companion whose voice was compared to the flutes of the family of Dawud (عليه السلام). :: Abu Musa al-Ash'ari (رضي الله عنه) || H: Sahih al-Bukhari
The poet of the Prophet ﷺ who answered the poets of Quraysh. :: Hassan ibn Thabit (رضي الله عنه) || H: Sahih al-Bukhari
The Companion who conquered Egypt. :: 'Amr ibn al-'As (رضي الله عنه) || H: Tarikh al-Tabari
The young Companion appointed by the Prophet ﷺ to lead an army including senior Companions. :: Usamah ibn Zayd (رضي الله عنهما) || H: Sahih al-Bukhari
The first wife of the Prophet ﷺ, who supported him with her wealth. :: Khadijah (رضي الله عنها) || H: Sahih al-Bukhari
The Mother of the Believers who was a great scholar and narrator. :: 'A'ishah (رضي الله عنها) || H: Sahih al-Bukhari
The Mother of the Believers whose wise advice at al-Hudaybiyah helped the Muslims. :: Umm Salamah (رضي الله عنها) || H: Sahih al-Bukhari
The Mother of the Believers called "Umm al-Masakin" for her charity. :: Zaynab bint Khuzaymah (رضي الله عنها) || H: Sirat Ibn Hisham
The Ansari leader whose death shook the Throne. :: Sa'd ibn Mu'adh (رضي الله عنه) || H: Sahih al-Bukhari
The host of the Prophet ﷺ in Madinah, who died near Constantinople. :: Abu Ayyub al-Ansari (رضي الله عنه) || H: Siyar A'lam al-Nubala' (al-Dhahabi)
The first to recite the Qur'an aloud to Quraysh near the Ka'bah, according to Ibn Ishaq. :: 'Abdullah ibn Mas'ud (رضي الله عنه) || H: Sirat Ibn Hisham
The Companion of the Ghifar tribe known for zuhd, called "the most truthful in speech". :: Abu Dharr al-Ghifari (رضي الله عنه) || H: Sunan al-Tirmidhi

## 300
The Companion appointed governor of Kufah who founded its scholarship. :: 'Abdullah ibn Mas'ud (رضي الله عنه) || H: Tarikh al-Tabari
The Companion sent by the Prophet ﷺ as judge and teacher to Yemen. :: Mu'adh ibn Jabal (رضي الله عنه) || H: Sunan Abi Dawud
The Companion who was the first to be born to the Muhajirun in Madinah. :: 'Abdullah ibn al-Zubayr (رضي الله عنهما) || H: Sahih al-Bukhari
The mother of 'Abdullah ibn al-Zubayr. :: Asma' bint Abi Bakr (رضي الله عنها) || H: Sahih al-Bukhari
The Companion who was the Prophet's "keeper of secrets", who knew the names of the hypocrites. :: Hudhayfah ibn al-Yaman (رضي الله عنه) || H: Sahih al-Bukhari
The Companion of the Ansar given the name "Dhul-Shahadatayn" (his witness counts as two). :: Khuzaymah ibn Thabit (رضي الله عنه) || H: Sunan Abi Dawud
The Companion whom the angels washed after he fell at Uhud. :: Hanzalah ibn Abi 'Amir (رضي الله عنه) || H: al-Mustadrak (al-Hakim)
The Companion who carried the banner at Uhud until he was martyred. :: Mus'ab ibn 'Umayr (رضي الله عنه) || H: Sirat Ibn Hisham
The Companion, a former slave, whom the Prophet ﷺ adopted before adoption was ended. :: Zayd ibn Harithah (رضي الله عنه) || H: Qur'an 33:37; Sahih al-Bukhari
The Mother of the Believers whose marriage was ordered in the Qur'an. :: Zaynab bint Jahsh (رضي الله عنها) || H: Qur'an 33:37
The Mother of the Believers who was the daughter of 'Umar. :: Hafsah (رضي الله عنها) || H: Sahih al-Bukhari
The Mother of the Believers who gave her turn to 'A'ishah (رضي الله عنها). :: Sawdah bint Zam'ah (رضي الله عنها) || H: Sahih al-Bukhari
The Mother of the Believers who was the daughter of Abu Sufyan. :: Umm Habibah (Ramlah) (رضي الله عنها) || H: Sahih al-Bukhari
The Mother of the Believers from the Jewish tribe of Banu al-Nadir. :: Safiyyah bint Huyayy (رضي الله عنها) || H: Sahih al-Bukhari
The Mother of the Believers whose marriage freed many of her people, Banu al-Mustaliq. :: Juwayriyah bint al-Harith (رضي الله عنها) || H: Sunan Abi Dawud
The last wife the Prophet ﷺ married. :: Maymunah bint al-Harith (رضي الله عنها) || H: Sirat Ibn Hisham
The son of the Prophet ﷺ who died as a baby in Madinah. :: Ibrahim || H: Sahih al-Bukhari
The mother of Ibrahim, son of the Prophet ﷺ. :: Mariyah al-Qibtiyyah (رضي الله عنها) || H: Sirat Ibn Hisham
The eldest daughter of the Prophet ﷺ. :: Zaynab (رضي الله عنها) || H: Sirat Ibn Hisham
The four daughters of the Prophet ﷺ. :: Zaynab, Ruqayyah, Umm Kulthum, Fatimah (رضي الله عنهن) || H: Sirat Ibn Hisham

## 400
'Umar (رضي الله عنه) was martyred in this year. :: 23 AH / 644 CE || H: Tarikh al-Tabari
Abu Bakr (رضي الله عنه) died in this year. :: 13 AH / 634 CE || H: Tarikh al-Tabari
'Uthman (رضي الله عنه) was martyred in this year. :: 35 AH / 656 CE || H: Tarikh al-Tabari
'Ali (رضي الله عنه) was martyred in this year. :: 40 AH / 661 CE || H: Tarikh al-Tabari
Abu Bakr (رضي الله عنه) fought the wars against those who refused zakat and false prophets, called this. :: Hurub al-Riddah || H: Tarikh al-Tabari
The false prophet killed at the Battle of Yamamah. :: Musaylimah || H: Tarikh al-Tabari
The Companion who killed Musaylimah, with others. :: Wahshi ibn Harb (رضي الله عنه) || H: Sahih al-Bukhari
'Umar (رضي الله عنه) founded the register of stipends called this. :: al-Diwan || H: Tarikh al-Tabari
The city 'Umar (رضي الله عنه) entered peacefully and gave a covenant to its people. :: Jerusalem (Bayt al-Maqdis) || H: Tarikh al-Tabari
The great battle against the Byzantines in 15 AH / 636 CE. :: The Battle of Yarmuk || H: Tarikh al-Tabari
The great battle against the Persians in 15 AH / 636 CE. :: The Battle of al-Qadisiyyah || H?: Tarikh al-Tabari; reports give 14, 15 or 16 AH
The Companion who led at Yarmuk, with Khalid (رضي الله عنه). :: Abu 'Ubaydah ibn al-Jarrah (رضي الله عنه) || H: Tarikh al-Tabari
The plague in al-Sham in 18 AH / 639 CE in which Abu 'Ubaydah and Mu'adh (رضي الله عنهما) died. :: The plague of 'Amwas || H: Tarikh al-Tabari
The six-man council that 'Umar (رضي الله عنه) named to choose the next caliph. :: al-Shura || H: Sahih al-Bukhari
The Companion who ran the council and chose 'Uthman (رضي الله عنه). :: 'Abd al-Rahman ibn 'Awf (رضي الله عنه) || H: Sahih al-Bukhari
'Uthman (رضي الله عنه) bought this well and gave it to the Muslims. :: Bi'r Rumah || H: Sunan al-Tirmidhi; Sahih al-Bukhari (ta'liq)
'Ali (رضي الله عنه) moved the capital to this city. :: Kufah || H: Tarikh al-Tabari
The Companion who was the last Companion to die, by the common view. :: Abu al-Tufayl 'Amir ibn Wathilah (رضي الله عنه) || H: Muqaddimah Ibn al-Salah
The last Companion to die in Madinah, by one view. :: Sahl ibn Sa'd (رضي الله عنه) || H?: Muqaddimah Ibn al-Salah; to be checked
The Ansari woman who was the first to give allegiance at 'Aqabah with her husband, and fought at Uhud. :: Nusaybah bint Ka'b (رضي الله عنها) || H: Sirat Ibn Hisham

## 500
The Prophet ﷺ announced in Madinah, with tears, the deaths of these three on the day they fell at Mu'tah. :: Zayd, Ja'far and 'Abdullah ibn Rawahah (رضي الله عنهم) || H: Sahih al-Bukhari
Ja'far (رضي الله عنه) was given this title because he was given two wings in Paradise. :: al-Tayyar (Dhul-Janahayn) || H: Sahih al-Bukhari
The Companion with whom Zayd (رضي الله عنه) found the last verses of Surah al-Tawbah in writing. :: Abu Khuzaymah al-Ansari (رضي الله عنه) || H: Sahih al-Bukhari
The Companion with the most hadith from among the Ansar. :: Jabir ibn 'Abdullah (رضي الله عنهما) || H: Siyar A'lam al-Nubala' (al-Dhahabi)
The Companion called "Habr al-Ummah" (the scholar of the Ummah). :: 'Abdullah ibn 'Abbas (رضي الله عنهما) || H: Siyar A'lam al-Nubala' (al-Dhahabi)
The four 'Abdullahs (al-'Abadilah). :: Ibn 'Umar, Ibn 'Abbas, Ibn al-Zubayr, Ibn 'Amr ibn al-'As (رضي الله عنهم) || H: Muqaddimah Ibn al-Salah
The Companion who told a friend, "Sit with us so we can believe for an hour" (meaning). :: Mu'adh ibn Jabal (رضي الله عنه) || H: Sahih al-Bukhari, Book of Faith (ta'liq)
The Companion who said "I wish I were a tree that is cut down" (meaning) out of fear of Allah. :: Abu Dharr al-Ghifari (رضي الله عنه) || H?: Sunan al-Tirmidhi; wording to be checked
The Companion who was the first in Islam to be called "Amir al-Mu'minin". :: 'Umar ibn al-Khattab (رضي الله عنه) || H: Tarikh al-Tabari
The Companion governor of al-Sham under 'Umar and 'Uthman, later caliph. :: Mu'awiyah ibn Abi Sufyan (رضي الله عنهما) || H: Tarikh al-Tabari
The Companion who led the first Muslim navy, under 'Uthman (رضي الله عنه). :: Mu'awiyah (رضي الله عنه) || H: Tarikh al-Tabari
The Battle of Dhat al-Sawari in 34 AH / 655 CE was this kind of battle. :: A sea battle || H?: Tarikh al-Tabari; year given as 31 to 35 AH
The woman Companion who sailed in the first naval expedition, as the Prophet ﷺ foretold. :: Umm Haram bint Milhan (رضي الله عنها) || H: Sahih al-Bukhari
The Companion who conquered Persia's capital al-Mada'in. :: Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas (رضي الله عنه) || H: Tarikh al-Tabari
The Companion who founded Basrah as a garrison town. :: 'Utbah ibn Ghazwan (رضي الله عنه) || H: Tarikh al-Tabari
The Companion governor of Kufah who founded it as a garrison town. :: Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas (رضي الله عنه) || H: Tarikh al-Tabari
The Ansari Companion who was the "chief of the Khazraj". :: Sa'd ibn 'Ubadah (رضي الله عنه) || H: Sahih al-Bukhari
The book by Ibn 'Abd al-Barr رحمة الله عليه on the Companions. :: al-Isti'ab || H: al-Isti'ab
The book by Ibn al-Athir رحمة الله عليه on the Companions. :: Usd al-Ghabah || H: Usd al-Ghabah
