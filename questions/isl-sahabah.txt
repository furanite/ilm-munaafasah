# Every Companion's name carries (رضي الله عنه / عنها / عنهم). No Companion is ever drawn or voiced.

## 100
The first caliph of Islam. :: Our master Abu Bakr al-Siddiq (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهِ) || H: Sahih al-Bukhari
The second caliph, called al-Faruq. :: Our master 'Umar ibn al-Khattab (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهِ) || H: Tarikh al-Tabari
The third caliph, called Dhul-Nurayn. :: Our master 'Uthman ibn 'Affan (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهِ) || H: Tarikh al-Tabari
The fourth caliph, cousin and son-in-law of our Beloved Prophet ﷺ. :: Our master 'Ali ibn Abi Talib (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهِ) || H: Tarikh al-Tabari
The four caliphs together are called this. :: al-Khulafa' al-Rashidun || H: Sunan Abi Dawud (hadith of al-'Irbad)
The ten Companions given glad tidings of Paradise by name. :: al-'Asharah al-Mubashsharah || H: Sunan al-Tirmidhi
The Companion who gave the first call to prayer. :: Bilal ibn Rabah (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهِ) || H: Sunan Abi Dawud
The daughter of our Beloved Master ﷺ, wife of our master 'Ali (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهِ). :: Our lady Fatimah al-Zahra' (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهَا) || H: Sahih al-Bukhari
The two grandsons of our Beloved Prophet ﷺ. :: Our master al-Hasan and al-Husayn (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهِمَا) || H: Sahih al-Bukhari
Our Beloved Prophet ﷺ called them "the leaders of the youth of Paradise" (paraphrased). :: Our master al-Hasan and al-Husayn (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهِمَا) || H: Sunan al-Tirmidhi
The Companion called "Sayf Allah" (Sword of Allah). :: Khalid ibn al-Walid (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهِ) || H: Sahih al-Bukhari
The Persian Companion who searched for the truth across lands. :: Salman al-Farisi (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهِ) || H: Musnad Ahmad
The Companion called "Amin hadhihi al-Ummah" (trustee of this Ummah). :: Abu 'Ubaydah ibn al-Jarrah (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهِ) || H: Sahih al-Bukhari
The wives of our Beloved Master ﷺ are called this. :: Ummahat al-Mu'minin (Mothers of the Believers) || H: Qur'an 33:6
The young Companion who became a great scholar of tafsir. :: 'Abdullah ibn 'Abbas (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهِمَا) || H: Sahih al-Bukhari
Our Beloved Prophet's ﷺ servant for about ten years. :: Anas ibn Malik (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهِ) || H: Sahih Muslim
The Companion our Beloved Prophet ﷺ called "the most knowledgeable of halal and haram" (paraphrased). :: Mu'adh ibn Jabal (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهِ) || H: Sunan al-Tirmidhi
The uncle of our Beloved Prophet ﷺ called "Asad Allah" (Lion of Allah). :: Our master Hamzah (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهِ) || H: al-Mustadrak (al-Hakim)
The Companion who was the first ambassador of Islam, sent to teach Yathrib. :: Mus'ab ibn 'Umayr (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهِ) || H: Sirat Ibn Hisham
The caliph who had the Qur'an copied into one standard Mushaf and sent copies to the great cities. :: Our master 'Uthman ibn 'Affan (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهِ) || H: Sahih al-Bukhari
The son of our master 'Umar (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهِ), famed for following the Sunnah closely. :: 'Abdullah ibn 'Umar (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهِمَا) || H: Siyar A'lam al-Nubala' (al-Dhahabi)
The Companion whose footsteps our Beloved Prophet ﷺ heard ahead of Him ﷺ in Paradise (meaning). :: Bilal ibn Rabah (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهِ) || H: Sahih al-Bukhari

## 200
The ten promised Paradise include the four caliphs, Talhah, al-Zubayr, 'Abd al-Rahman ibn 'Awf, Sa'd, Sa'id and this one. :: Abu 'Ubaydah ibn al-Jarrah (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهِ) || H: Sunan al-Tirmidhi
The Companion who was the first to shoot an arrow in the way of Allah. :: Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهِ) || H: Sahih al-Bukhari
The commander of the Muslims at al-Qadisiyyah. :: Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهِ) || H: Tarikh al-Tabari
The Companion called the "disciple" (hawari) of our Beloved Master ﷺ. :: al-Zubayr ibn al-'Awwam (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهِ) || H: Sahih al-Bukhari
The wealthy Companion who began life in Madinah asking to be shown the market. :: 'Abd al-Rahman ibn 'Awf (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهِ) || H: Sahih al-Bukhari
The Companion who shielded our Beloved Prophet ﷺ with his hand at Uhud, which was left paralysed. :: Talhah ibn 'Ubaydullah (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهِ) || H: Sahih al-Bukhari
The Companion who was the scribe of revelation and collected the Qur'an. :: Zayd ibn Thabit (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهِ) || H: Sahih al-Bukhari
The Companion whose recitation our Beloved Prophet ﷺ loved and asked to hear. :: 'Abdullah ibn Mas'ud (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهِ) || H: Sahih al-Bukhari
The Companion whose voice was compared to the flutes of the family of Dawud (عليه السلام). :: Abu Musa al-Ash'ari (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهِ) || H: Sahih al-Bukhari
The poet of our Beloved Master ﷺ who answered the poets of Quraysh. :: Hassan ibn Thabit (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهِ) || H: Sahih al-Bukhari
The Companion who conquered Egypt. :: 'Amr ibn al-'As (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهِ) || H: Tarikh al-Tabari
The young Companion appointed by our Beloved Prophet ﷺ to lead an army including senior Companions. :: Usamah ibn Zayd (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهِمَا) || H: Sahih al-Bukhari
The first wife of our Beloved Prophet ﷺ, who supported Him ﷺ with her wealth. :: Our lady Khadijah (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهَا) || H: Sahih al-Bukhari
The Mother of the Believers who was a great scholar and narrator. :: Our lady 'A'ishah (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهَا) || H: Sahih al-Bukhari
The Mother of the Believers whose wise advice at al-Hudaybiyah helped the Muslims. :: Our lady Umm Salamah (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهَا) || H: Sahih al-Bukhari
The Mother of the Believers called "Umm al-Masakin" for her charity. :: Zaynab bint Khuzaymah (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهَا) || H: Sirat Ibn Hisham
The Ansari leader whose death shook the Throne. :: Sa'd ibn Mu'adh (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهِ) || H: Sahih al-Bukhari
The host of our Beloved Master ﷺ in Madinah, who died near Constantinople. :: Abu Ayyub al-Ansari (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهِ) || H: Siyar A'lam al-Nubala' (al-Dhahabi)
The first to recite the Qur'an aloud to Quraysh near the Ka'bah, according to Ibn Ishaq رحمة الله عليه. :: 'Abdullah ibn Mas'ud (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهِ) || H: Sirat Ibn Hisham
The Companion of the Ghifar tribe known for zuhd, called "the most truthful in speech". :: Abu Dharr al-Ghifari (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهِ) || H: Sunan al-Tirmidhi
The caliph who first had the Qur'an gathered into one set of pages after the Battle of Yamamah. :: Our master Abu Bakr al-Siddiq (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهِ) || H: Sahih al-Bukhari
Our Beloved Prophet ﷺ gave our master 'Ali (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهِ) this nickname, finding him lying in the dust. :: Abu Turab || H: Sahih al-Bukhari
The Rightly Guided caliphate would last this many years, as our Beloved Prophet ﷺ foretold (meaning). :: 30 || H: Sunan Abi Dawud; Sunan al-Tirmidhi (hadith of Safinah)

## 300
The Companion appointed governor of Kufah who founded its scholarship. :: 'Abdullah ibn Mas'ud (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهِ) || H: Tarikh al-Tabari
The Companion sent by our Beloved Prophet ﷺ as judge and teacher to Yemen. :: Mu'adh ibn Jabal (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهِ) || H: Sunan Abi Dawud
The Companion who was the first to be born to the Muhajirun in Madinah. :: 'Abdullah ibn al-Zubayr (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهِمَا) || H: Sahih al-Bukhari
The mother of 'Abdullah ibn al-Zubayr. :: Asma' bint Abi Bakr (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهَا) || H: Sahih al-Bukhari
The Companion who was our Beloved Prophet's ﷺ "keeper of secrets", who knew the names of the hypocrites (meaning). :: Hudhayfah ibn al-Yaman (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهِ) || H: Sahih al-Bukhari
The Companion of the Ansar given the name "Dhul-Shahadatayn" (his witness counts as two). :: Khuzaymah ibn Thabit (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهِ) || H: Sunan Abi Dawud
The Companion whom the angels washed after he fell at Uhud. :: Hanzalah ibn Abi 'Amir (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهِ) || H: al-Mustadrak (al-Hakim)
The Companion who carried the banner at Uhud until he was martyred. :: Mus'ab ibn 'Umayr (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهِ) || H: Sirat Ibn Hisham
The Companion, a former slave, whom our Beloved Prophet ﷺ adopted before adoption was ended. :: Zayd ibn Harithah (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهِ) || H: Qur'an 33:37; Sahih al-Bukhari
The Mother of the Believers whose marriage was ordered in the Qur'an. :: Zaynab bint Jahsh (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهَا) || H: Qur'an 33:37
The Mother of the Believers who was the daughter of 'Umar. :: Our lady Hafsah (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهَا) || H: Sahih al-Bukhari
The Mother of the Believers who gave her turn to our lady 'A'ishah (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهَا). :: Sawdah bint Zam'ah (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهَا) || H: Sahih al-Bukhari
The Mother of the Believers who was the daughter of Abu Sufyan. :: Umm Habibah (Ramlah) (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهَا) || H: Sahih al-Bukhari
The Mother of the Believers from the Jewish tribe of Banu al-Nadir. :: Safiyyah bint Huyayy (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهَا) || H: Sahih al-Bukhari
The Mother of the Believers whose marriage freed many of her people, Banu al-Mustaliq. :: Juwayriyah bint al-Harith (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهَا) || H: Sunan Abi Dawud
The last wife our Beloved Master ﷺ married. :: Maymunah bint al-Harith (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهَا) || H: Sirat Ibn Hisham
The son of our Beloved Prophet ﷺ who died as a baby in Madinah. :: Ibrahim || H: Sahih al-Bukhari
The mother of Ibrahim, son of our Beloved Prophet ﷺ. :: Mariyah al-Qibtiyyah (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهَا) || H: Sirat Ibn Hisham
The eldest daughter of our Beloved Master ﷺ. :: Zaynab (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهَا) || H: Sirat Ibn Hisham
The four daughters of our Beloved Prophet ﷺ. :: Zaynab, Ruqayyah, Umm Kulthum, our lady Fatimah (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهِنَّ) || H: Sirat Ibn Hisham
"Take the Qur'an from four": Ibn Mas'ud, Salim, Mu'adh and this Companion (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهِم) (meaning). :: Ubayy ibn Ka'b (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهِ) || H: Sahih al-Bukhari
The mother of Anas ibn Malik (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهِ), who gave her son to serve our Beloved Prophet ﷺ. :: Umm Sulaym (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهَا) || H: Sahih al-Bukhari
The poet who came to Madinah to seek pardon and recited the ode "Banat Su'ad". :: Ka'b ibn Zuhayr (رِضْوَانُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْهِ) || H: Sirat Ibn Hisham
