## 300
The number of patterns (bab) of the bare three-letter verb. :: 6 || H: Shadha al-'Arf
The first bab, "nasara yansuru", has these vowels on the middle letter. :: Fathah in the past, dammah in the present || H: Shadha al-'Arf
The bab "daraba yadribu" has these middle vowels. :: Fathah in the past, kasrah in the present || H: Shadha al-'Arf
The bab "fataha yaftahu" usually needs one of these letters in the root. :: A throat letter || H: Shadha al-'Arf
The bab "karuma yakrumu" shows this kind of meaning. :: A lasting quality || H: Shadha al-'Arf
The bab "hasiba yahsibu" has these middle vowels. :: Kasrah in both || H: Shadha al-'Arf
