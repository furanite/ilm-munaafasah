## 300
Intentionally breaking a Ramadan fast by marital relations requires this expiation. :: Kaffarah || H: Sahih al-Bukhari
The kaffarah is first freeing a slave, then fasting this many months in a row. :: Two || H: Sahih al-Bukhari
If one cannot fast the two months, one feeds this many poor people. :: 60 || H: Sahih al-Bukhari
In the Hanafi and Maliki schools, intentionally eating in Ramadan also requires this. :: Kaffarah (as well as qada') || H: Nur al-Idah; al-Risalah
In the Shafi'i and Hanbali schools, kaffarah in Ramadan is only for this. :: Marital relations || H: Matn Abi Shuja'; Zad al-Mustaqni'
The amount of zakat al-fitr in the hadith, of dates or barley. :: One sa' || H: Sahih al-Bukhari
In the Hanafi school, zakat al-fitr may be paid in this. :: Its value in money || H: Nur al-Idah; al-Hidayah
In the Shafi'i, Maliki and Hanbali schools, zakat al-fitr is paid in this. :: Staple food || H: Matn Abi Shuja'; Zad al-Mustaqni'
In the Hanafi school, half a sa' of this grain is enough for zakat al-fitr. :: Wheat || H: al-Hidayah (al-Marghinani)
The intention for the Ramadan fast, in the Shafi'i and Hanbali schools, must be made at this time. :: At night, before dawn || H: Matn Abi Shuja'; Zad al-Mustaqni'
In the Hanafi school, the intention for a Ramadan fast can be made until this time. :: Before midday (al-dahwah al-kubra) || H: Nur al-Idah (al-Shurunbulali)
In the Maliki school, one intention at the start of this is enough. :: The month of Ramadan || H: al-Risalah (Ibn Abi Zayd)
The least time for i'tikaf in the Hanafi school for a voluntary i'tikaf. :: Any time, even a moment || H: Nur al-Idah (al-Shurunbulali)
The sunnah i'tikaf in the last ten of Ramadan must be in this. :: A mosque || H: Qur'an 2:187
In the Hanafi school, a woman's i'tikaf is in this. :: The prayer place of her home || H: Nur al-Idah (al-Shurunbulali)
The Prophet ﷺ said Laylat al-Qadr should be sought in these nights (paraphrased). :: The odd nights of the last ten || H: Sahih al-Bukhari
The du'a the Prophet ﷺ taught 'A'ishah (رضي الله عنها) for Laylat al-Qadr asks for this. :: Pardon ('afw) || H: Sunan al-Tirmidhi
A pregnant or nursing woman who fears for herself or her child may do this. :: Break the fast and make it up || H: Sunan Abi Dawud; Sunan al-Tirmidhi
In the Shafi'i school, a nursing woman who fears only for the child makes up and also does this. :: Pays fidyah || H: Matn Abi Shuja'
The Prophet ﷺ forbade joining fasts day and night without eating, called this. :: Wisal || H: Sahih al-Bukhari

## 400

Cupping (hijamah) breaks the fast in this school. :: The Hanbali school || H: Zad al-Mustaqni'; Sunan Abi Dawud
In the Hanafi school, vomiting a mouthful on purpose does this. :: Breaks the fast || H: Nur al-Idah (al-Shurunbulali)
Vomiting by accident does this to the fast. :: Nothing || H: Sunan Abi Dawud; Sunan al-Tirmidhi

In the Hanafi school, fasting on the "day of doubt" (30 Sha'ban) for Ramadan is this. :: Disliked (makruh) || H: Nur al-Idah (al-Shurunbulali)

The Prophet ﷺ called the days of tashriq days of eating, drinking and this. :: Remembrance of Allah || H: Sahih Muslim

The Companion whom the Prophet ﷺ advised not to fast every day, but as Dawud fasted. :: 'Abdullah ibn 'Amr (رضي الله عنهما) || H: Sahih al-Bukhari
The Prophet ﷺ fasted most in this month outside Ramadan. :: Sha'ban || H: Sahih al-Bukhari
The amount of fidyah per day in the Hanafi school. :: Half a sa' of wheat (or its value) || H: al-Hidayah (al-Marghinani)
The amount of fidyah per day in the Shafi'i school. :: One mudd of staple food || H: Matn Abi Shuja'
The Hanafi school allows breaking a voluntary fast but then requires this. :: Making it up || H: Nur al-Idah (al-Shurunbulali)
In the Shafi'i school, breaking a voluntary fast without excuse is this. :: Allowed, with no make-up required || H: Matn Abi Shuja'
